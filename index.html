Our Story

A Farm Built on
Trust & Freshness

Nestled in the fertile heartland of Abakaliki, Ebonyi State — Nigeria's foremost agricultural hub — Halleluyah Farms and Agro has grown from a commitment to delivering farm produce of the highest possible standard.

We serve food suppliers, wholesalers, restaurants, and everyday buyers with the same unwavering dedication: fresh from the farm, reliably supplied, available around the clock.
